# Branding

## Name
**Bifrost Brews** — kept from the original repo, now representing a Gen AI
courses knowledge site + internal company forum instead of the original
mead/recipe blog.

## Naming logic
- **Bifrost** — the rainbow bridge in Norse mythology connecting Asgard to
  Midgard: a bridge between worlds/people. Maps naturally onto "bridging"
  learners to Gen AI knowledge, and coworkers to each other via the forum.
- **Brews** — something being steeped/crafted (knowledge "brewing"), and
  also evokes a café/pub — a casual gathering spot, which fits a forum for
  informal company chat/gossip alongside more serious course content.

## Tagline options

**Punchy / short (good under a logo or in a nav bar subtitle):**
1. "Where AI knowledge brews."
2. "Knowledge on tap."
3. "Brewing smarter minds."

**Combines both halves of the product (courses + forum):**
4. "Courses to learn. A bridge to talk it over."
5. "Learn AI. Brew conversation."
6. "Knowledge on tap, gossip on the side."
7. "Sip on Gen AI, spill the tea on everything else."

**Leans into the bridge/community angle:**
8. "The bridge between learning AI and talking about it."
9. "Where courses and conversation cross paths."

## Decision (2026-08-23)
The user picked **#5 "Learn AI. Brew conversation."** as the site tagline —
compact, and covers both halves of the product (courses + forum). Use it on
the home page hero; the other options above are kept for reference.
